Problem Statement:
-> Imagine you are building a system for a small parking lot.

🔹 1. Vehicle Entry
When a vehicle comes:
👉 System should:
Find an available parking spot
Assign that spot to the vehicle
Mark that spot as occupied

🔹 2. Vehicle Exit
When a vehicle leaves:
👉 System should:
Free the parking spot
Mark it as available again

🔹 3. Vehicle Types
We have:
Car 🚗
Bike 🏍️
👉 For now:
Don’t overcomplicate (no size constraints yet)

🔹 4. Parking Spots
Fixed number of spots
Each spot can be:
Empty
Occupied

🔹 5. Parking Strategy (IMPORTANT 🔥)
When a vehicle enters:
👉 How do we choose a spot?
We support:
Nearest available spot
Random spot
